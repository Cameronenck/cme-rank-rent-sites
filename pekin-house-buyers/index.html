Selling in Pekin

What’s Different About Selling Here

Pekin and Tazewell County have their own realities. Here’s what homeowners here often run into.

Older Housing Stock

Many Pekin homes were built decades ago. Buyers commonly flag aging roofs, foundations, and outdated electrical and plumbing. A cash offer accounts for those instead of asking you to fix them first.

Judicial Foreclosure

Illinois is a judicial foreclosure state — the lender files in circuit court, and the process can take many months to a year or more. If you’re behind, a cash sale can be a way to settle the loan and move on before it plays out.

Inherited Houses & Probate

An inherited home in Illinois goes through probate at the Tazewell County Courthouse in Pekin, which can take many months to a year or more. We can help you understand the timeline and buy once title is clear.

Tired Landlords

Illinois evictions can be slow — a contested case can take many months to a year or more. We buy rental properties with tenants in place, so you can sell with tenants in place rather than work through an eviction.

General information, not legal advice.

How It’s Calculated

How Your Cash Offer Is Calculated

Our offer is straightforward — here’s the math, in plain English.

  1. 1

    After-repair value (ARV)

    What your house would sell for after it’s fully fixed up — based on comparable recent sales nearby.

  2. 2

    Minus repair costs

    An estimate of what it would cost to bring the home to that finished condition — roof, systems, cosmetic work, and holding costs.

  3. 3

    Minus our costs

    Our resale and holding costs, plus a fair margin for taking on the work and risk.

  4. 4

    Your cash offer

    What’s left is the cash offer — the amount you’d receive, with no fees and no commissions taken out.

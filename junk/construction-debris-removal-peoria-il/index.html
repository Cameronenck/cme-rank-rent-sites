Construction Debris Removal in Peoria, IL

Renovation projects generate more debris than the original estimate accounts for. The drywall from a bathroom gut, the old cabinets from a kitchen remodel, the concrete chunks from a broken patio, the roofing shingles from a re-roof — none of it fits in a garbage bin, and most of it can't go to a standard disposal facility without a contractor account. If you're a homeowner who just finished a project, or a contractor who needs clean sites without renting and managing dumpsters, Peoria Junk Removal Pros handles debris pickup and disposal throughout Peoria County.

Contractor Debris Removal Service

General contractors and remodeling crews in Peoria County use us to keep job sites clean without the overhead of managing a dumpster — permits, placement, and swap scheduling. We can schedule recurring weekly pickups during a long project, or on-call same-day service when a phase finishes and you need the debris gone before the next trade comes in.

We don't require a contract for recurring service, but repeat contractors get priority scheduling. If you're running three remodeling projects in Peoria simultaneously and need debris cleared on demand, call us and we'll set up a working relationship.

What We Don't Take

Hazardous materials are outside our scope: asbestos-containing materials (ACM), lead paint debris requiring special abatement, and chemical waste. If your project involves pre-1980 materials and you suspect asbestos or lead, get a licensed abatement contractor involved before debris removal. We'll haul the clean debris after abatement is complete.

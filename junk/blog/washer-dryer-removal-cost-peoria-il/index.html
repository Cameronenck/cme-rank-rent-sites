What Affects Your Washer and Dryer Removal Cost?

  • Unit Size and Weight: Larger commercial-grade machines cost more to remove and transport than standard residential models. Heavy units need extra labor and equipment.
  • Disconnect Complexity: Washers require water supply and drain disconnection. Gas dryers need gas line shutdown. Electric dryers only need power disconnection. Complex plumbing adds $50–$75.
  • Access and Stairs: If machines are on an upper floor or in a tight basement with narrow doorways, removal becomes harder. Stairs or difficult access can add $50–$100.
  • Appliance Age and Scrap Value: Older machines are disposed as scrap. Newer machines in good condition may have salvage value. Some removal services offer $25–$50 credit for newer units.
  • Location: Rural areas around Peoria and Pekin may have slightly higher costs due to distance to disposal facilities. Urban Peoria proper typically has lower rates.
  • Replacement Set: If you need new washers and dryers delivered the same day, many removal services bundle the deal. Ask about removal + delivery discounts.

Pro Tip: Call before your service date. Some junk removal companies in Peoria check if your washer or dryer is salvageable. Newer units in working condition may be donated to local charities, and you might get a small credit or tax deduction letter.

When Should You Replace Your Washer or Dryer?

Modern washers and dryers last 10–15 years on average. If yours is older and breaking down regularly, removal and replacement often make financial sense. Here are the signs:

  • Age over 12 years
  • Frequent repairs costing $200+ per visit
  • Water leaks or rust spots
  • Dryer not heating or clothes taking 2+ cycles to dry
  • Loud grinding, squeaking, or vibration
  • Mold or mildew smell inside the machine

If you're upgrading, professional removal clears the old machines out so you can install new ones immediately. No waiting, no hassle.

How to Prepare for Washer and Dryer Removal

To keep your removal cost as low as possible and ensure we can remove your machines safely:

  • Empty the machines completely. No clothing, lint, or items left inside.
  • Let us know if machines are front-load or top-load. Affects how we position them for removal.
  • Clear the area around them. Move boxes, supplies, or other items so we can access them easily.
  • Turn off water and gas before we arrive. We can do it, but it speeds up the process if you prepare.
  • Let us know if you want the machines moved out quickly. If you're installing new ones same-day, we coordinate with the delivery team.

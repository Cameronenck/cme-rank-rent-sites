Shed Cleanout Cost in Peoria IL — Real Pricing

If you've got a shed in Peoria that's become a junk storage unit, you're not alone. Sheds start organized and slowly turn into dumping grounds for tools, seasonal items, old furniture, and things you keep "just in case."

A professional shed cleanout in Peoria IL runs $300–$700 depending on size and fullness. Here's the breakdown:

Typical Shed Cleanout Pricing

Small shed (8x10 or smaller, lightly filled): $250–$400

Medium shed (10x12, moderately full): $400–$550

Large shed (12x16 or bigger, heavily packed): $550–$800+

These prices include labor, hauling, sorting, and disposal. Your actual cost depends on how much junk is inside and what you want to keep.

Why Shed Cleanout Costs What It Does

Shed Size

A 10x10 shed holds roughly half the junk of a 12x16 shed. Bigger space = more volume = higher cost. We quote based on actual shed size and fullness.

How Full Your Shed Is

A shed packed floor-to-ceiling costs more than one with scattered junk. A full load might need multiple trips or a larger truck.

Condition of Items

Heavy or broken items take more labor to remove safely. Hazardous materials (paint, chemicals, fuel) cost extra or can't be taken at all.

Access and Location

Easy driveway access = faster work. If your shed is far from our truck or items have to be hand-carried through narrow gates, labor goes up.

How to Save Money on Your Shed Cleanout

Pre-Sort Valuable Items

Before we arrive, pull out anything you want to keep or sell (tools, sports equipment, seasonal decorations). This cuts down on what we haul and saves you money.

Donate Items Yourself

Good furniture or working appliances can go to Goodwill, Salvation Army, or local Peoria thrift stores. We'll haul what's left behind.

Schedule During Off-Peak Hours

Weekday cleanouts are sometimes cheaper than weekend availability. Ask about scheduling flexibility when you call for a quote.

Combine Multiple Jobs

If you also need a garage or basement cleaned out, bundle them into one call. Combined jobs often come with a better rate than doing them separately.

Shed Cleanout vs. DIY Removal

DIY costs: Rental dumpster ($400–$600+), multiple trips to the landfill, your labor, and time off work.

Professional cleanout: $300–$700 flat-rate, no dumpster hassle, no second-hand hauling, and your time stays yours.

For most Peoria homeowners, professional removal saves both money and stress.

What Gets Removed During a Shed Cleanout

We haul almost everything from a shed, including:

  • Old tools, hand tools, power tools (non-working or broken)
  • Wooden pallets, scrap wood, lumber scraps
  • Broken furniture, old chairs, tables
  • Metal scrap, old gates, shelving units
  • Garden equipment, wheelbarrows, rusty lawn mowers
  • Seasonal decorations, holiday items
  • Appliances (refrigerators, washers, dryers, water heaters)
  • Tires, rubber mats, worn-out hoses

We Cannot Remove

  • Paint or paint cans
  • Chemicals, fertilizers, pesticides
  • Fuel or propane tanks
  • Batteries (except basic household batteries)
  • Asbestos or hazardous insulation

If you have hazardous materials, ask when you call. We can often work around them or recommend a disposal specialist.

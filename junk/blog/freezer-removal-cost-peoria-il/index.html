Why Professional Removal Matters

You might be tempted to move a freezer yourself or post it on Facebook Marketplace for free. I get it—it's a big, heavy box. Here's why that often backfires:

Safety Risk: An upright freezer weighs 200–300 pounds. A chest freezer or deep freezer can hit 400+ pounds. Moving one down stairs, through a tight basement, or across a yard puts your back—and your property—at serious risk. I've seen people hurt doing this.

Environmental Law: Freezers and air conditioners contain refrigerant (usually R-134a or older CFC compounds). Dumping or improper disposal is illegal and damages the ozone layer. Landfills won't accept them, and residential trash pickup won't either. You need a licensed recycler.

Liability: If someone gets hurt removing or transporting your freezer, you're liable. If they take it to a junk site and someone else gets hurt with it, that can still come back on you. Professional removal protects you.

For $200–$250, professional removal is actually the cheapest option when you factor in your time, risk, and the liability headache.

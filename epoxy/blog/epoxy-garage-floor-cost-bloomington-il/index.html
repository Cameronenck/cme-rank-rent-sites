How Much Does an Epoxy Garage Floor Cost in Bloomington IL?

Published September 2026 7 min read Serving Bloomington, Normal & McLean County IL

If you have priced an epoxy garage floor in Bloomington IL, you have probably gotten quotes ranging from a few hundred dollars to well over six thousand for what sounds like the same job. The gap comes down to square footage, concrete condition, and which coating system is actually being quoted.

This guide breaks down real 2026 pricing for epoxy garage floor cost in Bloomington IL, what each tier includes, and the line items that move a quote up or down.

Short Answer: What Homeowners in Bloomington Are Paying

For a standard two-car garage, installed pricing in Bloomington and Normal generally lands in these ranges:

  • Entry-level epoxy (1 coat, minimal prep): $2,000 to $2,900
  • Mid-grade epoxy with flake broadcast (3 coat system): $3,100 to $4,400
  • Premium full-flake or metallic with polyaspartic topcoat: $4,600 to $7,500

Per square foot, that is roughly $4 to $12. A typical Bloomington two-car garage is 400 to 480 square feet, so most complete jobs land between $2,200 and $5,500.

Bloomington trends above bare-bones rates mainly because of concrete condition. Much of the housing stock here was built between the 1960s and 1990s, and older slabs frequently show scaling from winter salt and freeze-thaw cycling.

What Is Included at Each Price Tier

Tier 1: The Budget Quote ($4-$6 per sq ft)

A budget quote usually means acid etching instead of mechanical grinding, one coat of epoxy, and no topcoat. It can work on a brand-new, perfectly cured slab. On a 30-year-old Bloomington floor with salt damage, it is the installation most likely to peel within a few winters.

Tier 2: The Standard Residential Job ($6-$9 per sq ft)

This is where most Bloomington homeowners should be. It includes diamond grinding for proper surface profile, crack and spall repair, a base coat, a full flake broadcast, and a clear topcoat.

Tier 3: The Premium System ($9-$15 per sq ft)

Premium work adds a polyaspartic topcoat for UV stability and abrasion resistance, thicker millage, and often metallic finishes. If you park daily drivers or run a workshop, this is the tier that gets you there.

The Line Items That Change Your Quote

When you compare two bids for an epoxy garage floor in Bloomington IL, check whether each of these is included. Two quotes that look $800 apart are often not quoting the same scope.

  • Surface preparation: Diamond grinding and shot blasting cost more than acid etching, and they are the difference between a coating that bonds and one that lifts.
  • Crack and spall repair: Often billed per linear foot. Older Bloomington and Normal slabs commonly need $150 to $600 of this work.
  • Moisture testing: A calcium chloride or plastic sheet test shows whether the slab pushes vapor. If it does, a moisture-blocking primer is required.
  • Topcoat type: Polyaspartic or polyurea topcoats resist UV yellowing and foot traffic better than epoxy topcoats.

Why Bloomington Concrete Behaves Differently Than You Expect

McLean County sits in a freeze-thaw band that punishes concrete. A Bloomington garage slab cycles through dozens of freeze-thaw events each winter, and road salt tracked in on tires accelerates surface deterioration.

That means prep matters more here than in milder climates, because a weak, scaled top layer fails under any coating. A coating without a UV-stable topcoat will also amber out faster in a south-facing garage.

Pricing across Central Illinois is fairly consistent, but the differences matter if you are comparing quotes across city lines.

  • Bloomington and Normal IL: $4 to $12 per sq ft. Slightly higher due to older slab conditions. Newer Normal builds need moisture checks rather than heavy repair.
  • Peoria IL: $3.50 to $11 per sq ft. A larger contractor pool keeps the low end more competitive.
  • Morton and Washington IL: $4 to $12 per sq ft. In line with the regional average.

Epoxy Garage Floor vs. Painted Concrete: Is the Extra Cost Worth It?

We get this question from almost every Bloomington homeowner. Concrete paint is a film-forming coating that sits on the surface. It does not bond the way epoxy does, and it cannot handle hot tire pickup, the most common failure point in a garage. Paint typically needs redoing every two to four years in an active garage.

Epoxy bonds mechanically to a properly profiled slab and handles hot tire pickup, chemical drips, and abrasion far better, holding up eight to twelve years. Over fifteen years, paint costs more in labor and materials.

Questions to Ask Before You Sign a Bloomington Quote

  1. Will you diamond grind or shot blast, or are you acid etching?
  2. What is the total system thickness in mils?
  3. Is a moisture test included before install?
  4. Are crack and spall repairs included in this number?
  5. What topcoat are you using, and is it UV stable?
  6. What does the warranty actually cover, and for how long?

A contractor who cannot answer these clearly is not a contractor you want pouring a three-coat system on a slab you will live with for the next decade.

Is There a Best Time of Year to Install in Bloomington?

Epoxy needs stable temperatures to cure properly, which in Central Illinois means late spring through early fall. Applying in an unheated garage in January is a recipe for a coating that never fully cures.

September and October are some of the best months for a Bloomington project. Humidity drops, temperatures stay workable, and the floor gets sealed before salt season starts.

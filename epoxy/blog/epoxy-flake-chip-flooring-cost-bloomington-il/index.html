How Much Does Flake Epoxy Flooring Cost in Bloomington IL?

Published July 2026 • 8 min read • By Peoria Epoxy Flooring Experts

Flake Epoxy Flooring Pricing in Bloomington IL

If you're looking at decorative flooring options for your Bloomington home or business, flake epoxy (also called chip epoxy or broadcast chip epoxy) is one of the most popular choices we install in the area. It's durable, attractive, comes in dozens of color combinations, and hides imperfections far better than solid-color epoxy. But what does it actually cost in Bloomington?

Short answer: Flake epoxy flooring in Bloomington typically runs $2.50 to $4.50 per square foot installed, depending on the color flake package you choose and whether you add a polyaspartic topcoat. For a typical two-car garage (400–500 sq ft), expect to pay between $1,000 and $2,250 for the base epoxy with flakes, or $1,500 to $3,000 if you add a protective polyaspartic seal on top.

Breaking Down the Cost: Base Epoxy + Flakes + Topcoat

Flake epoxy installation is a three-part process, and the cost reflects each stage. Here's what you're actually paying for in Bloomington:

1. Concrete Preparation ($300–$600)

Before any epoxy touches your garage floor, we grind and profile the concrete to remove old paint, sealers, dirt, and any slick spots. In Bloomington's climate (freeze-thaw cycles, salt exposure, moisture), this step is critical—skipping it leads to premature peeling. A 400–500 sq ft garage typically takes 2–3 hours of grinding time. Some installers want to bundle this into per-square-foot pricing; we charge it separately so you know exactly what goes into surface prep.

2. Base Epoxy + Flake Broadcast ($1.50–$2.50/sq ft)

The epoxy base coat and colored vinyl flakes make up the bulk of the material cost. A quality two-part epoxy system with broadcast flakes runs roughly $1.50 to $2.50 per square foot. For a 450 sq ft garage, that's $675 to $1,125 in materials and labor for the epoxy and flakes.

The flakes themselves come in dozens of color combinations: classic black base with white or blue flakes, gray base with charcoal, custom blends, and multicolor mixes. More decorative or custom blends can add $0.25–$0.50/sq ft to the material cost.

3. Topcoat (Optional but Recommended) ($0.75–$1.50/sq ft)

The real durability boost comes from a polyaspartic topcoat sealed over the flakes. This adds $0.75 to $1.50 per square foot, but extends the lifespan of your floor from 4–6 years to 7–10 years in Bloomington conditions. It also increases chemical resistance (oil, salt, de-icer) and adds slip resistance. For a 450 sq ft garage, add $340 to $675 for a quality topcoat.

Flake Epoxy vs. Solid-Color Epoxy: What's the Difference in Price?

You might be wondering: why not just go with solid-color epoxy and save money? Both are durable, but flake epoxy costs more and offers real advantages:

Solid-color epoxy: $1.50–$2.50/sq ft installed. Looks clean and modern. Shows dust, oil stains, and concrete cracks more visibly. Typical lifespan with topcoat: 6–8 years in Bloomington.

Flake (chip) epoxy: $2.50–$4.50/sq ft installed. Decorative appearance, much better at hiding imperfections, stains, and dirt. The flakes add visual depth and texture. Slip resistance built in. Typical lifespan with topcoat: 7–10 years.

In Bloomington, where garages often get muddy and dusty from yard work and equipment, flake epoxy makes more sense. The extra $0.50–$1.00/sq ft buys you a floor that looks good longer and hides the reality of a working garage.

What Affects Flake Epoxy Costs in Bloomington?

Several factors push your final cost up or down:

  • Square footage: Larger projects cost less per square foot because prep and labor scale. A 300 sq ft garage costs more per sq ft than a 500 sq ft garage.
  • Slab condition: If your garage floor has deep cracks, spalling, or moisture problems, prep work increases. That's on top of the per-sq-ft cost.
  • Color choice: Standard colors (black base, gray base) are baseline pricing. Custom or premium color flakes add $0.25–$0.50/sq ft.
  • Topcoat selection: Polyaspartic (premium) costs more than polyurethane or no topcoat. Polyaspartic is worth it in Bloomington's climate.
  • Warranty: We offer a 2-year warranty on flake epoxy with topcoat. Longer warranties (5+ years) typically mean higher upfront cost but better peace of mind.
  • Timeline: Expedited installation (same-week turnaround) may carry a rush fee. Standard scheduling is built into the quote above.

Why Choose Flake Epoxy for Your Bloomington Home?

Bloomington homeowners choose flake epoxy for three main reasons:

1. Durability in Midwest Weather — Bloomington winters mean freeze-thaw cycles, salt, and moisture. A quality epoxy with flakes and a polyaspartic topcoat handles this far better than paint or thin coatings. You're looking at 7–10 years of life, vs. 2–3 years for floor paint.

2. Low Maintenance — Flake epoxy resists oil stains, dust, and dirt better than solid colors. A quick sweep and mop every week keeps it looking sharp. No sealing or waxing needed if you've got a polyaspartic topcoat.

3. Resale Appeal — If you ever sell your Bloomington home, a finished garage floor with flake epoxy adds value and catches buyer eyes. It signals care and investment in the property.

Pro Tip for Bloomington Customers: If you're considering flake epoxy for your garage, ask about adding anti-UV protection. Bloomington summers have intense sun, and UV can fade the color over time. UV-resistant topcoats cost a bit more upfront but keep your floor looking vibrant for years longer.

FAQ: Flake Epoxy Flooring in Bloomington

How long does flake epoxy take to cure in Bloomington weather? ▶

Flake epoxy typically takes 24–48 hours for foot traffic and 3–7 days before you can park a car on it. Bloomington's humidity and temperature affect cure time—warmer, drier conditions speed it up; cooler, wetter conditions slow it down. We'll give you an exact timeline based on conditions when we do your installation.

Can flake epoxy be installed in basements in Bloomington? ▶

Yes, but moisture is the biggest concern in Bloomington basements due to groundwater and humidity. We use moisture barriers and epoxy systems designed for basements. It costs more upfront ($3–$5/sq ft) but prevents the peeling and bubbling you'd get if you skipped the moisture management.

Do you warranty flake epoxy floors in Bloomington? ▶

Yes. We offer a 2-year warranty on material and workmanship for flake epoxy with topcoat. The warranty covers peeling, significant fading (excluding normal wear), and adhesion failure. Normal wear, floor traffic, and cleaning are not covered. Ask about extended warranty options when you get your quote.

What's the best way to maintain flake epoxy floors in Bloomington? ▶

Sweep regularly, mop with pH-neutral cleaner monthly, and avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive scrubbers. Every 2–3 years, consider a topcoat refresh to maintain gloss and UV protection. That's it. No waxing, no special treatments—just basic cleaning keeps it looking new for years in Bloomington conditions.

Can I DIY flake epoxy or should I hire a pro in Bloomington? ▶

DIY is possible but risky. Concrete prep is the hardest part—you need the right grinder, experience reading the concrete, and safety equipment. Epoxy application requires speed (flakes must be broadcast into wet epoxy) and even distribution. A bad application fails in 2–3 years. Professional installation ensures proper prep, even flake coverage, and a warranty. For Bloomington homes, we recommend hiring a pro.

Are flake epoxy floors slippery? ▶

Flake epoxy is more slip-resistant than solid-color epoxy because the vinyl flakes add texture. However, a wet flake epoxy floor can still be slippery. Most topcoats include anti-slip aggregate or a textured finish. We can add extra slip resistance if needed—important for basements, bathrooms, or commercial spaces in Bloomington.

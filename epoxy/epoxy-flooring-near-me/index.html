LOCAL KNOWLEDGE

Why Peoria’s Freeze-Thaw Cycles Destroy Untreated Concrete Floors

Central Illinois winters are hard on concrete. Here’s what homeowners are dealing with across Peoria County — and how epoxy addresses each problem.

Freeze-Thaw Spalling

Peoria averages 18–22 freeze-thaw cycles per winter. Water penetrates uncoated slabs, freezes, expands, and pops the surface. Epoxy seals the pores and stops the cycle.

De-Icing Salt Damage

Road salt tracked in from Illinois highways accelerates concrete degradation. Epoxy creates a chemical-resistant barrier that keeps salt and melt products from attacking the slab.

Older Slab Construction

Many Peoria homes from the 1950s–1980s have lower-strength slabs poured without vapor barriers. Moisture vapor drive can be significant — we test for it before any coating goes down.

Hot Summer Expansion

Peoria summer temps regularly exceed 90F. UV-unstable coatings yellow and delaminate. We exclusively use UV-stable polyurethane topcoats that maintain color and adhesion through heat cycles.

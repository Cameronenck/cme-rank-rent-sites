Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about epoxy flooring services in Peoria, IL

How much does epoxy flooring cost in Peoria IL?
Garage floor epoxy in Peoria typically runs $3 to $7 per square foot for a full system including prep, base coat, broadcast layer, and topcoat. A standard 2-car garage ranges from $1,200 to $2,800.
How long does epoxy flooring last?
A properly installed 3-coat epoxy system with a polyurethane or polyaspartic topcoat typically lasts 10 to 20 years in a residential garage with normal use.
How long does installation take?
Most residential garage floors take 2 to 3 days. Day one is surface prep and repairs, day two is coating application, and the floor is ready for foot traffic in 24 hours and vehicles in 72 hours.
Can epoxy be applied over existing concrete?
Yes, as long as the concrete is structurally sound. We diamond-grind the surface to create a proper mechanical bond. Badly damaged or moisture-compromised slabs may need repairs first.
Is epoxy flooring slippery when wet?
A smooth epoxy surface can be slippery when wet. We add anti-slip aggregate to the topcoat for garage and commercial floors, providing excellent traction even when wet.
What is the difference between epoxy and polyaspartic?
Epoxy is the base coating that bonds to concrete and provides thickness. Polyaspartic is a UV-stable topcoat that cures faster and resists yellowing. We often combine both for the best results.
Do you coat basement floors?
Yes. We install epoxy systems in basements, laundry rooms, and workshops. Moisture testing is performed first to ensure proper adhesion.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Peoria, East Peoria, Pekin, Morton, Washington, Bartonville, Dunlap, Chillicothe, Germantown Hills, Peoria Heights, and Creve Coeur.
Can I choose custom colors and flake patterns?
Absolutely. We offer a full range of solid colors, metallic finishes, and decorative flake blends. We bring samples to your consultation so you can see options in your space.
How do I maintain an epoxy floor?
Epoxy floors are very low maintenance. Sweep regularly and mop with a mild cleaner as needed. Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive scrubbers. The topcoat resists stains from oil, chemicals, and road salt.

Insulation Matters in Lancaster County

Lancaster County winters regularly push below freezing from December through February. A non-insulated steel door over an attached garage means your utility bill climbs every winter as cold air seeps through. We recommend insulated doors (R-value of 6 or higher) for any attached garage, especially if there's living space above.

  • Non-insulated steel: lowest cost, fine for detached garages or mild climates
  • Single-layer insulated (R-6): good balance of cost and thermal performance
  • Double-layer insulated (R-13 to R-18): best for attached garages with living space above

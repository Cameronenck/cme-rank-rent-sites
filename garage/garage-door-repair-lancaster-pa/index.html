Common Garage Door Repairs We Handle

Most garage door problems fall into a handful of categories. Here's what we see most often in Lancaster County and what it typically involves:

  • Broken or bent panels: Panel damage from backing into the door, storm debris, or just age. Single-panel replacement if the rest of the door is in good shape. Full replacement if structural damage is too extensive.
  • Off-track doors: A door that's jumped its tracks won't open safely. We re-align and inspect the tracks, rollers, and cables that caused it — not just push it back on and call it done.
  • Snapped cables: Cables carry the counterbalance load of your door. When one snaps, the door drops unevenly and can damage the opener. We replace cables in pairs to keep tension balanced.
  • Worn or broken rollers: Rollers wear out over time — usually every 5–7 years depending on use. Worn rollers make the door loud and eventually cause it to stick or jump the track.
  • Damaged hinges: Hinges crack and fatigue from constant flexing. We replace steel or heavy-gauge hinges to restore smooth movement and prevent panel stress.
  • Bottom seal replacement: The rubber bottom seal keeps cold air, water, and critters out. Lancaster winters are hard on rubber — we replace deteriorated seals quickly.
  • Opener sensor issues: Photo-eye sensors that are misaligned or dirty prevent the door from closing. A 5-minute fix we handle properly, with instructions on prevention.

Garage Door Opener Installation in Lancaster PA

Whether your opener has stopped working, you're replacing an old noisy chain-drive unit, or you're upgrading to a smart opener you can control from your phone, we handle all opener work across Lancaster County. We supply and install, or install a unit you've already purchased — your choice.

Opener Types We Install

Chain Drive

$250–$350

Most affordable. Reliable and durable. Louder than belt drive — fine for a detached garage, less ideal if there's living space above.

Belt Drive

$300–$420

Quietest operation. Best choice when the garage is attached or below a bedroom. Most popular choice for Lancaster County attached garages.

Smart Wi-Fi Opener

$350–$450

Control from your phone anywhere. Get alerts when the door opens. Works with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit on most models.

Brands We Install

  • Chamberlain: Good mid-range option. Excellent app integration with myQ platform.

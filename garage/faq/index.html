If the door is less than 15 years old and damage is limited to springs, cables, rollers, or the opener, repair almost always makes more financial sense. If panels are severely dented, warped, or there is major structural damage to the door frame, replacement may cost less over the long run. We give you an honest assessment and never push for replacement when repair will do.
Standard torsion springs are rated for approximately 10,000 cycles — one cycle equals one open and one close. For a typical household that opens and closes the door 4 times per day, springs last roughly 7 to 9 years. High-cycle springs rated for 25,000 or 50,000 cycles are available and can last 15 to 20 years or more.
We strongly advise against DIY spring replacement. Garage door torsion springs are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury if handled incorrectly. The job requires specialized tools and training. Professional spring replacement is affordable and typically completed within an hour.

Common causes of noisy garage doors include worn rollers (especially nylon rollers that have hardened), loose hardware, dry hinges and bearings that need lubrication, worn-out springs, or an opener chain that needs tensioning. Most noise issues can be resolved with a tune-up that includes lubrication, hardware tightening, and roller inspection.

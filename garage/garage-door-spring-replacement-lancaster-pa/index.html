Garage Door Spring Replacement in Lancaster PA

Broken torsion or extension springs are the most common reason a garage door won't open in Lancaster County. Springs are under extreme tension, so when one snaps — often overnight in cold weather when steel contracts — the door stops working entirely. You may hear a loud bang and find the door stuck when you go to leave in the morning.

We stock springs sized for standard residential doors and for the non-standard openings common in Lancaster County's older homes. We replace springs in pairs so the door tension remains balanced, extend the inspection to cables and hardware while we're in there, and confirm the door operates safely before we leave.

Torsion Springs vs. Extension Springs

Most Lancaster County homes built after 1990 have torsion springs — a single spring mounted above the door on a metal shaft, or two springs for heavier double doors. These are more durable and safer when they fail. Older homes often have extension springs on each side of the door, which are less expensive to replace but need safety cables to prevent the spring from becoming a projectile if it breaks.

  • Torsion spring replacement: More precise fit required based on door weight. We measure your specific door before ordering.
  • Extension spring replacement: Typically replaced in pairs with safety cables. Common on homes built before 1985.
  • High-cycle springs: Available for doors used more than 5–6 times per day. Rated for 30,000+ cycles vs. standard 10,000.

Why Springs Fail in Lancaster County

Lancaster winters are hard on garage door springs. Steel contracts in cold temperatures, which adds extra stress to springs that are already cycling hundreds of times per year. A spring that's borderline in fall often fails on the first cold snap in January. We recommend inspection in October if your springs are more than 7 years old.

The typical torsion spring is rated for about 10,000 cycles — roughly 3–5 years of daily use for a household with two cars. We can upgrade to high-cycle springs during replacement if your door sees heavy traffic.

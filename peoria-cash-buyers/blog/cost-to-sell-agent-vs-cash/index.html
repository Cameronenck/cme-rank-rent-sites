Cost to Sell Your House: Agent vs. Cash Buyer

Where the money actually goes when you list with an agent, versus when you sell for cash.

The two paths, side by side

When you sell a house, you’re really choosing between two paths. Listing with an agent aims for the highest possible sale price, but it comes with costs and time. Selling to a cash buyer trades a bit of that top-line price for certainty, speed, and fewer out-of-pocket costs. Neither is “better” — the right choice depends on your house and your situation.

Where the money goes with an agent

  • Commission — typically 5–6% of the sale price, split between the listing and buyer’s agents.
  • Repairs and preparation — many sellers paint, repair, and stage before listing to compete.
  • Closing costs — the seller typically pays a share of title, escrow, and transfer costs.
  • Holding costs — mortgage, taxes, insurance, and utilities for every month the house sits on the market.

On a $150,000 sale, a 6% commission alone is $9,000 — before repairs, staging, or closing costs.

Where the money goes with a cash sale

  • No commission — you sell directly to us, so there’s no agent fee.
  • No repairs — we buy as-is, in the house’s current condition.
  • Closing costs covered — we pay them.
  • No holding costs — you can close in as little as 7 days instead of waiting months.

Time matters, too

A listed home often takes 30 to 90 days or more to go under contract, plus the closing period — and that assumes the buyer’s financing doesn’t fall through. A cash sale closes in as little as a week, with no financing contingency to worry about.

Which one is right for you?

If your home is in great shape, you have time, and you want to maximize the sale price, listing may be worth the costs. If you’d rather skip the repairs, the showings, and the uncertainty — or you need to sell on a specific timeline — a cash offer is worth a look. The good news: getting a cash offer is free and carries no obligation, so you can compare it directly against what an agent projects and decide with real numbers in front of you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about deck building and repair in Peoria, IL

How much does a new deck cost in Peoria IL?
A new pressure-treated wood deck in Peoria typically costs $25 to $45 per square foot installed. Composite decking runs $40 to $75 per square foot. A standard 300 sq ft deck ranges from $7,500 to $22,500 depending on materials and complexity.
How long does it take to build a deck?
Most standard decks take 1 to 2 weeks from start to finish. Complex multi-level designs with built-in features may take 3 to 4 weeks. Permit processing adds additional lead time.
Do I need a permit to build a deck in Peoria?
Yes. Most deck construction in Peoria and surrounding areas requires a building permit. We handle the permit application process for you as part of our standard service.
What is better: wood or composite decking?
Pressure-treated wood costs less upfront but requires annual staining and maintenance. Composite decking costs more initially but is virtually maintenance-free and resists rot, insects, and fading for 25+ years.
How long does a deck last in Central Illinois?
A well-maintained pressure-treated wood deck lasts 15 to 25 years. Composite decks last 25 to 50 years. Illinois freeze-thaw cycles make proper footing depth and drainage essential for longevity.
Can you repair my existing deck instead of replacing it?
Often, yes. We repair damaged boards, replace rotted joists, reinforce railings, and re-stain existing decks. We provide an honest assessment of whether repair or replacement makes more financial sense.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Peoria, East Peoria, Pekin, Morton, Dunlap, Washington, Metamora, and Chillicothe.
Do you offer deck staining and sealing?
Yes. We offer professional deck staining, sealing, and refinishing for both new and existing decks. We use commercial-grade stains that hold up to Central Illinois weather.
What is the best time of year to build a deck?
Spring and fall are ideal for deck construction in Peoria. Summer works well too but book early as schedules fill fast. We can pour footings and frame in cooler months as long as the ground is not frozen.

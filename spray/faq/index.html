Common questions about spray foam insulation in Peoria, IL

How much does spray foam insulation cost in Peoria IL?
Open-cell spray foam typically runs $0.50 to $1.00 per board foot, and closed-cell runs $1.25 to $2.00 per board foot in the Peoria area. A typical attic job ranges from $1,500 to $4,000 depending on square footage.
What is the difference between open-cell and closed-cell spray foam?
Open-cell foam is lighter, more flexible, and excellent for soundproofing. Closed-cell foam is denser, provides a vapor barrier, adds structural rigidity, and has a higher R-value per inch. We recommend the right type based on your application.
How long does spray foam insulation last?
Spray foam insulation is designed to last the lifetime of your home. Unlike fiberglass batts, it does not sag, settle, or lose R-value over time.
Is spray foam insulation safe?
Once fully cured (typically 24 hours), spray foam is inert and non-toxic. We follow all EPA and manufacturer safety protocols during installation and ventilate the space thoroughly.
Can spray foam be installed in existing walls?
Yes. We inject foam through small access holes drilled between studs, then patch and seal them. This is common in older Peoria homes that need insulation upgrades without full wall removal.
Will spray foam reduce my energy bills?
Most homeowners see 30 to 50 percent reduction in heating and cooling costs after spray foam installation. The air-sealing properties eliminate drafts that account for most energy loss.

How long does installation take?
Most residential attic or crawl space jobs are completed in one day. Larger whole-home projects may take 2 to 3 days.
Does spray foam help with moisture and mold?
Closed-cell spray foam acts as a vapor barrier and prevents condensation that leads to mold growth. It is especially effective in crawl spaces and basements common in Central Illinois homes.

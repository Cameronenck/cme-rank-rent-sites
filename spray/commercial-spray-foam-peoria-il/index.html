Why Spray Foam for Commercial Buildings in Peoria

Commercial buildings present energy challenges residential projects don't. Large footprints mean more exposure area. Metal construction means thermal bridging through every stud and purlin. Loading docks and frequent door openings create persistent infiltration points. Spray foam is one of the few insulation systems that addresses all of these simultaneously.

For metal buildings specifically — a common construction type in Peoria's industrial corridor and agricultural areas — closed-cell spray foam applied directly to the interior of the metal panels is far superior to fiberglass batt systems that rely on friction fit and lose effectiveness as metal buildings shift and settle over time.

Do you work on metal buildings in the Peoria area?
Yes — metal building insulation is one of our most common commercial applications. Closed-cell spray foam applied directly to metal roof and wall panels eliminates condensation, stops thermal bridging, and dramatically reduces heating and cooling loads. We work on agricultural, industrial, and commercial metal buildings throughout Peoria County.
Can you work around our business hours?
Yes. We can phase projects, work nights and weekends, or section off portions of an active facility to minimize operational disruption. We discuss scheduling requirements upfront so there are no surprises.
Are you licensed and insured for commercial work in Illinois?
Yes. We carry full commercial general liability insurance and all applicable Illinois licensing. We can provide certificates of insurance directly to your GC, property manager, or lender as needed.
How does commercial spray foam compare to fiberglass for a metal building?
Fiberglass batt systems for metal buildings rely on friction fit between framing members and lose effectiveness as the building moves and settles. They don't address thermal bridging through the metal frame. Spray foam adheres to the metal surface, creates a continuous thermal and air barrier, and eliminates the condensation that corrodes metal panels from the inside.
Do you handle permitting for commercial projects?
We can assist with documentation needed for permits, including R-value calculations and product data sheets. Permitting requirements vary by municipality — we're familiar with Peoria, East Peoria, and surrounding Peoria County jurisdictions.

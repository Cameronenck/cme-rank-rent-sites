High-Performance Building Envelopes

The building envelope — the thermal and air barrier between conditioned and unconditioned space — is the foundation of an energy-efficient home. Spray foam outperforms fiberglass batts in every measurable category relevant to a Peoria climate:

  • Air sealing — spray foam is both an insulator and an air barrier in a single application. No separate air barrier step, no tape failures, no gaps around electrical boxes and plumbing penetrations
  • R-value stability — unlike fiberglass, spray foam doesn't settle or compress over time. The R-value you pay for today is the R-value you have in 30 years
  • Moisture management — closed-cell foam is a Class II vapor retarder, providing moisture protection without a separate vapor barrier in most wall assemblies
  • Structural contribution — closed-cell foam adds measurable racking strength to wall panels, which can simplify or reduce structural sheathing requirements depending on your engineer's design

Common New Construction Applications

  • Exterior wall cavities — typically open-cell between studs, sometimes closed-cell for higher R-values in 2x4 walls
  • Unvented roof decks — closed-cell to the underside of roof sheathing for cathedral ceilings and conditioned attic designs
  • Rim joists and band joists — closed-cell applied during framing before subfloor goes in
  • Garage walls and ceiling (when conditioned) — open or closed-cell depending on design
  • Slab edge insulation — closed-cell on foundation walls before backfill

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the lead time to schedule new construction insulation in Peoria?
We typically need 5–10 business days notice for residential new construction jobs. For production builders with multiple units, we build your schedule into our recurring calendar. Contact us early in your project so we can plan accordingly.
Do you offer blower door testing coordination?
We don't perform blower door testing ourselves, but we work regularly with energy raters and third-party inspectors in Peoria County and can coordinate timing around testing requirements if your project needs a specific ACH50 result.
Can spray foam help us achieve energy code compliance in Illinois?
Yes. Illinois adopted the 2021 IECC, which has more stringent air sealing and insulation requirements than previous codes. A properly designed spray foam system — especially for the attic and rim joists — makes it significantly easier to hit the required ACH50 target at blower door testing.
Do you work with spec home builders as well as custom builders?
Yes. We work with both. For spec and production builders, reliable scheduling and consistent pricing are the priority. For custom builders, it's often more about performance specs and working around complex designs. We handle both.
What areas do you serve for new construction work?

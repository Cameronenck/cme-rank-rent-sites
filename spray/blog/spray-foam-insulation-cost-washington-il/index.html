Spray Foam Insulation Cost in Washington IL: What Homeowners Should Expect to Pay

Published: September 2026 | Reading time: 8 min | Washington, Illinois

For Washington, Illinois homeowners planning an insulation upgrade, the first question is usually the cost. Spray foam delivers outstanding air sealing and energy efficiency, but it carries a higher upfront price than fiberglass or blown-in cellulose. This guide breaks down realistic spray foam pricing in Washington IL so you can budget and compare quotes with confidence.

What Determines Spray Foam Insulation Cost in Washington IL

No two Washington homes are alike, and neither are their quotes. The final price is driven by a handful of variables every contractor weighs before giving a number:

  • Foam type: Open-cell and closed-cell carry very different material costs.
  • Application area: Attics, walls, crawl spaces, and rim joists each require different material and labor.
  • Square footage and depth: More surface area and a thicker application mean more board feet of foam.
  • Site accessibility: Tight crawl spaces and steep rooflines add labor time and cost.
  • Prep work: Removing old insulation or repairing framing adds to the total.

Average Spray Foam Insulation Costs in Washington IL

Spray foam is typically priced by the board foot, which is one square foot of coverage at one inch of thickness. In Washington IL, homeowners can generally expect these ranges for a typical residential project:

Project Type Typical Cost Range
Rim joist sealing (open-cell) $1,500 - $3,000
Attic (open-cell, roof deck) $3,000 - $7,000
Exterior walls (closed-cell) $4,000 - $12,000
Crawl space encapsulation $3,000 - $8,000

These are ballpark figures for a mid-size single-family home; your quote depends on your home's dimensions and the foam depth needed to hit your target R-value.

Pro Tip: Ask whether a quote is priced by board foot or by square foot of coverage. A board-foot quote accounts for thickness, while a square-foot quote usually assumes a standard depth. Make sure you are comparing apples to apples.

Open-Cell vs Closed-Cell Spray Foam: Cost Comparison

The biggest cost decision in a spray foam project is which foam type you choose. The two options perform and are priced differently.

Open-Cell Spray Foam Cost

Open-cell foam is the more affordable option, typically $0.90 to $1.50 per board foot in Washington IL. It expands to roughly 100 times its liquid volume, seals air effectively, and offers an R-value of about 3.5 to 3.9 per inch, making it a strong choice for attics and interior walls.

Closed-Cell Spray Foam Cost

Closed-cell foam is denser and more expensive, generally $1.50 to $3.00 per board foot. In exchange, it delivers an R-value of around 6.5 to 7.0 per inch and acts as a vapor barrier, the preferred option for crawl spaces, rim joists, and exterior walls in Central Illinois.

Spray Foam Cost Per Square Foot

When contractors quote by the square foot rather than the board foot, the numbers shift. At a one-inch depth, open-cell spray foam typically runs $0.44 to $0.65 per square foot, while closed-cell runs roughly $1.00 to $2.00 per square foot. Because most projects call for two to three inches of foam, that figure multiplies quickly.

Energy Savings and Long-Term Value

Many Washington IL homeowners choose spray foam despite the higher upfront cost because of the return on investment: proper installation reduces air leakage and can lower heating and cooling costs over the life of the home, especially through Central Illinois winters and humid summers.

Because spray foam does not settle or degrade like blown-in insulation, its R-value stays consistent for decades, making it a strong value per year of service.

How to Get an Accurate Spray Foam Estimate in Washington IL

The most reliable way to pin down your cost is an in-person or detailed remote assessment. To keep quotes accurate and comparable:

  1. Measure your space: Have rough square footage for the attic, walls, or crawl space you want insulated.
  2. Know your target R-value: Illinois climate zones typically call for attic R-values of R-49 to R-60 and wall values around R-13 to R-20.
  3. Specify the foam type: Ask for open-cell and closed-cell pricing so you can compare side by side.
  4. Get multiple quotes: Three written estimates give you a realistic picture of the going rate in Washington IL.

Common Questions About Spray Foam Insulation Cost

Is spray foam insulation worth the higher upfront cost?

For most Washington IL homeowners, yes. The superior air sealing and long-term durability typically offset the higher initial price through lower energy bills over time.

How much does spray foam cost per board foot in Illinois?

Open-cell foam generally runs $0.90 to $1.50 per board foot, while closed-cell runs $1.50 to $3.00 per board foot. Regional labor rates can shift these figures slightly.

Does closed-cell foam cost more than open-cell?

Yes, closed-cell foam is denser, provides a higher R-value per inch, and costs roughly 50% to 100% more than open-cell on a board-foot basis.

Can I insulate just the rim joist to save money?

Yes, rim joist sealing is one of the most cost-effective spray foam projects available, often $1,500 to $3,000 with meaningful air-sealing benefits.

What is Spray Foam Insulation?

Spray foam insulation is a liquid that expands and hardens into a solid, air-sealing foam. It's applied directly to walls, ceilings, and other surfaces via a spray application. Unlike traditional batts or blow-in insulation, spray foam expands to fill every cavity and crack, creating an airtight seal.

There are two main types of spray foam used for garage insulation in Macomb, IL:

Open-Cell Spray Foam

Open-cell foam has a softer, more flexible structure. It's less expensive than closed-cell and provides an R-value of about 3.5 per inch. It's a good choice if budget is a concern, though it absorbs moisture and isn't ideal for areas prone to water intrusion.

Closed-Cell Spray Foam

Closed-cell foam is denser and more rigid. It delivers an R-value of about 6.5 per inch and provides superior air sealing and moisture resistance. It's the premium choice for Macomb garages, especially if you want maximum energy efficiency and protection.

Spray Foam Garage Insulation vs. Other Options

You might be wondering how spray foam compares to other insulation methods. Here's the breakdown:

Spray Foam vs. Fiberglass Batts

Fiberglass batts are cheaper upfront ($200-$400 for a 2-car garage), but they leave gaps at edges and don't provide an air seal. Spray foam costs more but delivers superior air sealing and energy performance.

Spray Foam vs. Blown-In Insulation

Blown-in insulation is faster to install and less expensive than spray foam, but it settles over time and doesn't seal air leaks. Spray foam provides better long-term performance.

The Application Process

Here's what to expect when a Macomb spray foam contractor insulates your garage:

  1. Inspection: The contractor assesses your garage structure and identifies areas to be sprayed.
  2. Preparation: The crew protects fixtures, lights, and other items that shouldn't be sprayed.
  3. Spray Application: Spray foam is applied to walls, ceilings, and rim joists. It expands and hardens within hours.
  4. Trimming: Once cured, excess foam is trimmed flush with the framing.
  5. Finish: Drywall or other finishes can be applied over the foam for a clean look.

The entire process typically takes 1-3 days, depending on garage size and complexity.

Common Questions About Garage Spray Foam Insulation

How long does spray foam last?

High-quality spray foam can last 80+ years. It doesn't degrade, settle, or lose its R-value over time like other insulation materials.

Is spray foam safe?

Yes, when installed by certified professionals. Once cured, spray foam is inert and doesn't off-gas. During application, proper ventilation is important.

Can you finish over spray foam?

Absolutely. Once the foam hardens, you can drywall, panel, or paint over it for a finished look.

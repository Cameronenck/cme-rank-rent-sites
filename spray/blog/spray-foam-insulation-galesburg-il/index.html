Open-Cell vs. Closed-Cell Spray Foam for Galesburg Homes

When considering spray foam insulation in Galesburg, you have two main options. Understanding the difference helps you make the right choice for your property.

Open-Cell Spray Foam

R-Value: R-3.5 to R-3.8 per inch

Cost: $0.44–$0.65 per board foot

Best For: Interior walls, attics in non-humid climates

Benefits: More affordable, allows moisture vapor to pass, excellent sound dampening

Drawback: Lower R-value; not recommended for crawl spaces or areas with moisture exposure

Closed-Cell Spray Foam

R-Value: R-6 to R-7.5 per inch

Cost: $1.00–$2.00 per board foot

Best For: Crawl spaces, basements, band boards, exterior walls

Benefits: Superior R-value, vapor-resistant, structural strength, pest resistant

Drawback: Higher cost; may require ventilation in certain applications

For most Galesburg properties, a combination approach works best: closed-cell foam in crawl spaces and band boards where moisture is a concern, and open-cell foam in attics where cost-efficiency is the priority.

Spray Foam Insulation Applications in Galesburg

Attic Insulation

Your attic is the number-one source of heat loss in winter and heat gain in summer. Spray foam insulation on the underside of your roof deck or on the attic floor creates a complete thermal barrier. Galesburg homes with spray foam attics see energy savings of 25–35% on heating and cooling bills.

Crawl Space Encapsulation

Open crawl spaces in Galesburg basements are a major energy liability. Closed-cell spray foam applied to crawl space walls and band boards seals out cold air infiltration, prevents moisture issues, and protects against pests. Many Galesburg homeowners report a noticeable reduction in cold floors and basement dampness after spray foam encapsulation.

Basement Rim Joists

The rim joist—where the foundation meets the band board—is a thermal weak point. Spray foam insulation here prevents air leaks and cold transfer through concrete. This is one of the most cost-effective foam applications and delivers measurable comfort improvements in Galesburg basements.

Basement Walls

Full basement wall insulation with closed-cell spray foam is ideal for finished basements. It provides superior insulation, eliminates condensation, and prevents outside air from seeping through concrete cracks and gaps.

Band Board & Mudsill

The band board is a common air infiltration point. Spray foam sealing here reduces drafts, improves comfort, and prevents moisture and pest entry—a quick win for many Galesburg homes.

Common Questions About Spray Foam in Galesburg

Is spray foam safe?

Yes, when installed by professionals. The foam cures completely and becomes inert—it does not off-gas or pose health risks after installation. Professional installers use proper equipment and safety protocols.

How long does spray foam last?

High-quality spray foam can last 80+ years. It doesn't settle, compress, or lose R-value over time the way fiberglass does. Your investment protects your Galesburg home for decades.

Will spray foam affect my attic ventilation?

Proper spray foam installation actually improves attic conditions. Closed-cell foam on the roof deck creates a conditioned attic space. Open-cell foam requires proper ventilation, which your contractor will assess during the inspection.

Can I do this myself?

Professional-grade spray foam equipment is expensive and dangerous without training. DIY kits exist but deliver poor results and uneven coverage. The cost savings never offset the performance loss. Hire a professional for guaranteed results.

What about fiberglass or blown-in insulation instead?

Both are cheaper upfront but inferior for air sealing. Fiberglass batts have seams and gaps—air leaks right through. Blown-in settles over time and loses effectiveness. Spray foam fills every gap and maintains its R-value forever.

## 3 realistic local backlink ideas

1. **ProsperPeoria free listing** (do-follow) — submit both brands under "cash home buyers"/"we buy houses". $0, no approval needed. Do first.
2. **Peoria Area Chamber membership** — join as "TruOffer LLC dba Peoria Cash Buyers" (nofollow link but referral + local legitimacy; ~$450/yr — needs Cam's spend approval).
3. **Local data/content pitch** — publish a genuinely useful local piece (e.g. "How long a Peoria home takes to sell in 2026" with our own deal data) and pitch it to a local outlet (Peoria Journal Star community, HOI community blog) or a REIA (Central Illinois Real Estate Investors) newsletter for an editorial backlink. Requires a real, citable data point — no fabricated stats.

## Order of execution (at Step 3, after domains + numbers + git deploy)

1. ProsperPeoria (free, do-follow) — day 1.
2. Bing Places + Apple Business Connect + Facebook (free, need phone) — day 1–3 once REsimpli numbers exist.
3. Standard citations (Yelp, YP, Superpages, Manta, Foursquare, Hotfrog, Cylex, Brownbook) — batch, once NAP is settled.
4. BBB + Chamber — only if Cam approves the spend (recurring — needs his explicit OK).

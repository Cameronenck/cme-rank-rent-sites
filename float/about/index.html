About Lancaster Float Company

Lancaster Float Company started the way most small businesses do — from a genuine love of the craft and a desire to make something beautiful for people on their most important days. What began as creating balloon arches for family and friends grew into a full event styling business serving Lancaster County and surrounding communities.

We're based right here in Lancaster County and every installation is personally handled — from the initial design conversation to the moment we pack up after teardown. You won't deal with a large company dispatcher or wonder who's actually showing up to your event. You'll know exactly who you're working with.

Our specialty is organic balloon installations — arches, garlands, and custom styled setups that look elevated and intentional. We follow the trends in event decor closely and work to bring current, relevant aesthetics to Lancaster County events rather than recycling tired styles that have run their course.

We believe the decor at an event matters. It shapes how the space feels, it influences how guests photograph the moment, and it's often what families remember long after the party's over. That's why we approach every installation — from a small birthday party garland to a full wedding arch — with the same care and attention.

If you're planning an event in Lancaster County, we'd love to be part of it. Call 717-537-0549 and tell us what you're planning.

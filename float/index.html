Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
For weddings and large events, we recommend booking 4–8 weeks in advance, especially during peak season (May through October). For smaller events like birthday parties and baby showers, 2–3 weeks is typically enough. We do occasionally have last-minute availability, so it's always worth reaching out even on short notice.
What's included in the price?
All of our packages include design consultation, balloon materials, delivery, professional installation, and teardown after your event. We don't charge separately for setup or removal — that's built into the quote. We'll give you a full itemized quote before you commit to anything.
Do you handle setup and teardown?
Yes, always. We arrive before your guests do, set everything up, and return at the end of your event to take everything down. You don't have to worry about a thing. Just enjoy your event and we'll handle the rest.
Can I customize colors and styles?
Absolutely — that's the whole point. Every installation is custom built for your event. We work with your color palette, theme, venue, and style preferences. If you have inspiration photos from Instagram or Pinterest, send them along and we'll work from those. No templates here.
What areas do you serve?
We primarily serve Lancaster County including Lancaster, Lititz, Ephrata, Manheim, Mount Joy, Elizabethtown, Strasburg, Columbia, and Millersville. We also travel to York, Harrisburg, and Lebanon for larger events. If you're not sure if we cover your area, just ask — we're happy to check.
How much do balloon arches cost?
Balloon arch pricing depends on size, style, and complexity. A basic 6-foot balloon arch typically starts around $150–$200. Larger organic arches for weddings or major events can range from $300 to $600 or more depending on the design. We always provide a detailed quote before you commit.

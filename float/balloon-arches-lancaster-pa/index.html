Custom Balloon Arches for Every Event in Lancaster County

A balloon arch does something no other decor element quite manages: it defines space and creates a focal point that draws every eye in the room. Whether it's framing a ceremony entrance at a Lancaster barn venue, marking the start of a dessert buffet at a birthday party in Lititz, or creating a branded photo moment at a corporate event downtown, a well-designed arch transforms the look and feel of the entire space.

At Lancaster Float Company, we design and install custom balloon arches for events throughout Lancaster County and surrounding areas. Every arch is built to fit your specific event — your colors, your venue, your vision — and we handle everything from delivery through teardown so you can focus on your guests.

Types of Balloon Arches We Build

Organic Balloon Arches

The most popular style — varied balloon sizes arranged in a natural, flowing shape. No two are identical. Works beautifully in earth tones, muted palettes, and modern color combinations. Our most-requested installation for weddings and upscale events.

Classic Round Arch

A traditional arch with uniform balloon sizing and a symmetrical shape. Clean, polished, and timeless. Works well for corporate events, graduations, and spaces that call for a more structured aesthetic.

Half Arch / Garland Arch

A one-sided installation that anchors at one point and cascades across a backdrop, table, or entryway. Great for photo backdrops, dessert tables, and spaces where a full arch isn't practical.

Column Arches

Two balloon columns flanking an entrance or stage, connected by a banner or swag. Impactful from a distance. Common for graduation parties, outdoor events, and grand entrances where you want a statement piece visitors see before they arrive.

What Makes a Great Balloon Arch Installation

The difference between a professional balloon arch and a DIY one is in the details: consistent balloon sizing, proper armature and anchoring that holds through an entire event, thoughtful color distribution, and scale that fits the space. We've installed arches in Lancaster County venues with 8-foot ceilings and in barn spaces with 25-foot clearance. The design approach is different for each, and that's exactly the kind of judgment that comes from experience in local venues.

For outdoor events in Lancaster — and we see them often during Pennsylvania's warm months — we account for wind exposure, sun direction, and the heat that builds through a summer afternoon. Balloon arches can hold up beautifully outdoors with the right construction technique, but they need to be built and anchored differently than indoor installations. We talk through these variables on every quote call.

Balloon Arch Questions

How tall can the balloon arch be?
We build arches from about 6 feet up to 12+ feet for high-ceiling venues. The right height depends on your venue and the scale of the space. We'll recommend dimensions when we see the venue specs or visit the space.
Can I choose my own colors?
Always. Every arch is custom to your color palette. We carry a wide range of latex colors. If you have specific colors or inspiration images, share them when you call and we'll design around your vision.
How long before the event do you arrive?
We schedule installation to finish before your guests arrive. For most arches, we arrive 1.5–2 hours before the event start time. For larger or more complex installations, we'll arrive earlier.
Do you do outdoor balloon arches?
Yes, with the right anchoring and planning. Outdoor arches require wind-rated support and should be installed the same day as the event. We'll talk through your outdoor setup when you call.

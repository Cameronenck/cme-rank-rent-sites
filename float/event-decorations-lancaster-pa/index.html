Custom Event Styling in Lancaster, PA

Not every event fits neatly into a category. Engagement parties, bridal showers, retirement celebrations, anniversary parties, holiday events, sweet sixteens, quinceañeras — each has its own aesthetic requirements and deserves an installation designed for the specific celebration. Lancaster Float Company designs and installs custom balloon decor for events of all kinds throughout Lancaster County.

Events We Love Working On

Bridal Showers

Bridal showers deserve the same styling attention as the wedding itself. Romantic palettes, lush garlands, and photo backdrops that match the wedding aesthetic create a cohesive celebration arc from shower to reception.

Engagement Parties

The first celebration in the wedding journey. Often more relaxed in vibe, but still worth doing beautifully. We design engagement party installations that feel festive without being over-the-top.

Anniversary Parties

Milestone anniversaries — 25th, 50th — are major family celebrations. We design installations that feel elegant and meaningful, not generic.

Holiday & Seasonal Events

Holiday parties, New Year's celebrations, and seasonal events can incorporate balloon decor beautifully. Seasonal color palettes and themed installations add a professional touch.

Our Custom Design Process

For non-standard events, we start with a conversation. What's the occasion? What's the venue? What colors are you working with? Is there an existing aesthetic you want to match — florals, linens, invitations? Do you have inspiration images? We ask these questions because the answers shape the entire design.

The best installations feel like they belong to the event — they complement the other design elements rather than competing with them. That takes conversation and some back-and-forth, and we're happy to put in that time to get it right.

Planning Something Special?

Tell us about your event and we'll design something beautiful. No event is too specific or unusual.

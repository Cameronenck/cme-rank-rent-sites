Water Filtration FAQ

Common questions about water filtration, softeners, and water quality in Peoria IL

Is Peoria tap water safe to drink?
Peoria municipal water meets EPA safety standards, but it can contain chlorine, chloramine, and trace contaminants from aging infrastructure and agricultural runoff. Many homeowners install point-of-use filtration for better taste and additional protection, especially in homes with older pipes that may contribute lead.
What is the difference between a water softener and a water filter?
A water softener removes calcium and magnesium that cause hard water scale on fixtures and appliances. A water filter removes contaminants like chlorine, sediment, lead, and pesticides. Many Peoria homes benefit from both since Central Illinois groundwater is naturally hard and treated with chlorine.
How much does a whole-house water filtration system cost in Peoria?
Whole-house filtration systems typically range from $800 to $2,500 installed, depending on the technology and flow rate your home requires. Water softeners run $900 to $2,200 installed. Under-sink reverse osmosis units cost $400 to $900. We provide a written estimate before any work begins.
How long does water filtration installation take?
Most whole-house filter and softener installations are completed in three to five hours. Under-sink reverse osmosis units typically take one to two hours. We schedule around your availability and leave the work area clean.
Do I need a water test before installing a filtration system?
Yes, and we provide one free of charge. Every home has different water quality depending on the source, pipe age, and local conditions. Testing first ensures we recommend only the system your water actually needs rather than overselling equipment you do not need.
What does a water test check for?
Our free in-home water test measures hardness, pH, chlorine levels, total dissolved solids, iron content, and other common indicators. If the initial test suggests further analysis is needed, we can send a sample to a certified lab for a full panel including lead, nitrates, and pesticides.
How often do water filters need to be replaced?
It depends on the system and your water usage. Whole-house sediment pre-filters typically last 3 to 6 months. Carbon filters last 6 to 12 months. Water softener resin lasts 10 to 15 years with proper maintenance. Reverse osmosis membranes last 2 to 3 years. We provide a maintenance schedule specific to your system at installation.
Is Peoria water hard?
Yes. Central Illinois groundwater passes through thick limestone deposits, picking up calcium and magnesium. Peoria water typically tests between 15 and 25 grains per gallon, which is considered hard to very hard. This causes scale buildup on faucets, showerheads, and water heaters, and shortens the life of appliances.
Do you service well water systems?
Yes. We install and maintain filtration systems for well water homes throughout the Peoria area. Well water often requires additional treatment for iron, manganese, bacteria, and nitrates. We test your well water first to determine exactly what treatment is needed.

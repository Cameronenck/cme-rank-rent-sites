Deck and Patio Cleaning in Lancaster County

Lancaster's warm, humid summers are ideal for deck use — and ideal for algae growth. Shaded decks and patios in Lititz, Manheim, and Elizabethtown can develop a visible green film within a single season. Wooded lots in Ephrata and Columbia accumulate tannin stains from leaf decomposition that turn wood gray and discolored over time.

We clean wood decks, composite decks, concrete patios, paver patios, and natural stone surfaces throughout Lancaster County. Each surface type requires a different approach — the same pressure and cleaning solution that works on concrete will damage a composite deck. We match the method to the material every time.

Wood Deck Cleaning

Pressure washing wood decks requires restraint. Too much pressure opens wood grain and causes fuzzing, splintering, and premature weathering. We use lower pressure with a wide-fan nozzle and follow the wood grain to clean effectively without damaging the surface. Pre-treating with a wood-safe cleaning solution breaks down algae, mold, and tannin staining before the rinse pass.

A clean deck also prepares the surface properly for staining and sealing — if you're planning to refinish your deck this season, our cleaning service is a logical first step. We recommend letting the wood dry for 48–72 hours after washing before applying any stain or sealer.

Composite Deck Cleaning

Composite decks require soft washing — high-pressure washing can void manufacturer warranties and damage the surface finish. We use low-pressure application of composite-safe cleaning solutions to remove algae, mold, and grime without harming the material. Many Lancaster homeowners are surprised how much cleaner their composite deck looks after a proper soft wash.

Patio and Stone Surface Cleaning

Concrete patios handle higher pressure well. Paver patios require more care — high pressure can displace joint sand and damage the finish on certain paver types. We assess your specific paver type before starting, use appropriate pressure, and restore joint sand after cleaning if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is pressure washing safe for my composite deck?
High-pressure washing can damage composite decking and void manufacturer warranties. We soft-wash composite decks using low-pressure application with composite-safe cleaning solutions. The result is a thorough clean without any surface damage.
Should I clean my deck before staining or sealing?
Yes — stain and sealer won't bond properly to a dirty or algae-covered surface. A professional cleaning removes all organic growth and opens the wood grain so the finish can penetrate correctly. Allow 48–72 hours drying time after washing before applying any stain or sealer.
How often should I wash my deck in Lancaster PA?
Most Lancaster County decks benefit from an annual cleaning, especially those with nearby trees or significant shade. Decks in full sun may only need cleaning every two years. Regular cleaning extends the life of your deck surface significantly.
Can you clean natural stone patios?
Yes. Natural stone varies in porosity and hardness, so we assess your specific stone type before choosing the appropriate pressure and cleaning solution. Bluestone, travertine, and limestone all require different handling than concrete pavers.
Do you clean fence panels and railings?
Yes — fence washing and railing cleaning are common add-ons to deck and patio jobs. Pricing is based on linear feet of fence or railing. We can clean wood, vinyl, and aluminum fence panels.

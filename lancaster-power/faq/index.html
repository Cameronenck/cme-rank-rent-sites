What is soft washing and why is it better for some surfaces?
Soft washing uses low pressure combined with eco-friendly cleaning solutions to safely clean delicate surfaces like stucco, roof shingles, and painted wood. It kills biological growth at the root rather than just rinsing surface dirt.
How often should I power wash my house?
Most Lancaster County homes benefit from a wash every 1 to 2 years. Shaded homes and north-facing walls accumulate mold and algae faster due to Pennsylvania's humid summers.
Is power washing safe for stucco and brick?
High-pressure washing can damage old mortar and stucco. We use soft washing for these heritage surfaces, using low pressure with cleaning solution that lifts stains without damaging the material.
Can power washing damage my deck or siding?
Improper technique can cause damage. Our technicians adjust pressure, nozzle distance, and cleaning agents for each surface type to ensure safe, thorough cleaning without etching or gouging.
Do you clean roofs?
Yes. We use a soft wash method specifically designed for asphalt shingles and slate. This removes black algae streaks and moss without voiding your roof warranty.

How long does a typical power washing job take?
A standard house wash takes 1 to 3 hours. Driveways and patios take 30 minutes to 1 hour. Larger properties or multiple-service jobs may take a half day.
Do I need to be home during the service?
Not necessarily. As long as we have access to an outdoor water spigot and the areas to be cleaned, we can complete the job while you are away.
Is your cleaning solution safe for plants and pets?
Yes. We use biodegradable, eco-friendly cleaning solutions. We also pre-wet and rinse surrounding plants as an extra precaution during every job.

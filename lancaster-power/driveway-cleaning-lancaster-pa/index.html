Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing for Driveways

Concrete and asphalt driveways can handle high-pressure washing — these surfaces are dense enough to withstand the pressure needed to break up embedded stains. Pavers and stamped concrete require more care; we adjust our technique and pressure to match the surface so we clean thoroughly without disturbing joint sand or etching the finish.

For heavy oil staining, we pre-treat with a commercial degreaser before washing. This breaks down petroleum-based stains chemically, so the pressure washing step removes what's already been lifted rather than trying to blast it out mechanically.

How Often Should You Clean Your Driveway?

Most Lancaster County driveways benefit from a professional cleaning every one to two years. Driveways with heavy vehicle traffic, nearby trees, or shaded sections that accumulate moss and algae may need annual attention. A clean driveway also makes your whole property look better-maintained — it's one of the highest-visibility improvements you can make to your home's exterior.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will pressure washing damage my concrete driveway?
Not when done correctly. We use appropriate pressure settings for your surface type. Concrete in good condition handles high-pressure washing without issue. Damaged concrete — cracks, spalling, or exposed aggregate — requires more care. We assess the surface before starting and adjust technique accordingly.
Can you remove old oil stains from my Lancaster driveway?
Yes, in most cases. Fresh oil stains respond better than old, set-in stains. We pre-treat oil staining with a commercial degreaser before washing. Very old or deeply penetrated stains may lighten but not disappear completely — we'll give you an honest expectation during the estimate.
Do you clean asphalt driveways?
Yes. Asphalt requires lower pressure than concrete to avoid surface damage, especially on older or sealer-treated asphalt. We adjust our settings and technique for asphalt surfaces.
How long does driveway cleaning take?
Most residential driveways in the Lancaster area take one to two hours. Longer or heavily stained driveways may take longer. We'll give you a time estimate when we assess the job.

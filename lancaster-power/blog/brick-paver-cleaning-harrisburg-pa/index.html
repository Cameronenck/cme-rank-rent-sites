Why Brick and Pavers Need Special Care

Unlike concrete, brick and paver surfaces are porous and vulnerable to pressure and harsh chemicals. High-pressure washing can:

  • Break down the mortar joints between bricks
  • Damage the surface sealant, accelerating weathering
  • Force water deep into the pores, leading to freeze-thaw damage in Lancaster winters
  • Strip away the protective patina that gives brick its character

This is why Harrisburg homeowners often benefit from a different approach than standard concrete cleaning.

Pressure Washing vs Soft Washing: When to Use Each

Quick Answer: Soft washing is the safer, preferred method for brick and pavers. Pressure washing is only appropriate for heavily soiled concrete or very tough buildup—never on aged brick.

Soft Washing Brick and Pavers

Soft washing uses low pressure (under 500 PSI) combined with biodegradable cleaning solutions to remove dirt, algae, mold, and stains without damaging the surface. This is the method professional contractors use for brick restoration in Harrisburg.

Best for:

  • Historic brick homes and older structures
  • Decorative patio pavers
  • Permeable pavers (which can trap water with high pressure)
  • Areas with delicate mortar or grout lines
  • Surfaces with moss, algae, or mold stains

Why it works: The low pressure allows the cleaning solution to do the work, dissolving organic growth and oxidation rather than blasting it away. The result is a clean, fresh surface with zero risk of damage.

Pressure Washing Brick and Pavers

Traditional pressure washing (2000+ PSI) is too aggressive for most brick and paver applications. It's appropriate only in rare cases:

  • New construction cleanup on heavy-duty surfaces
  • Extreme cases of industrial staining (rare in residential)
  • Very thick cement buildup on heavy-duty stone

Why it's risky: Even at lower pressures, traditional washing can damage brick grout, discolor the surface, and compromise the structural integrity of older mortar. In the Harrisburg area, where freeze-thaw cycles accelerate moisture damage, this risk is especially high.

Cleaning Different Brick and Paver Surfaces

Historic Brick Homes in Harrisburg

Older brick in Harrisburg often has softer mortar (lime-based) that cannot tolerate any pressure. Professional contractors should assess the mortar condition first and typically use hand-scrubbing combined with low-pressure soft wash only.

Never attempt DIY pressure washing on historic brick—the cost of re-pointing damaged mortar can exceed $10,000+.

Brick Driveways and Pavers

Residential brick driveways are often less delicate than facades but still require care. Soft washing is still preferred, though slightly higher pressure (up to 1500 PSI) may be acceptable for newer, harder-fired brick in exceptional cases.

For permeable paver driveways (popular for drainage), soft washing is a must—high pressure can dislodge pavers and allow water infiltration underneath.

Brick Patios and Walkways

Patio pavers typically benefit from soft washing combined with sealing afterward. This restores the color and texture while protecting against future staining and weather damage in Harrisburg's variable climate.

DIY Brick Cleaning vs Professional Services

While some homeowners attempt DIY brick cleaning, the risks often outweigh the savings:

  • Equipment costs: Soft-wash equipment isn't cheap, and pressure washers are easy to misuse
  • Chemical safety: Wrong cleaning solutions can cause irreversible discoloration or efflorescence (white salt staining)
  • Property damage: A single mistake can cost thousands in repairs—one wrong pressure setting on historic brick can require full re-pointing
  • Time investment: Professional soft washing saves days of labor and delivers superior results

For brick and paver surfaces, professional cleaning in Harrisburg is almost always the smarter choice.

Spring Power Washing Guide for Hershey PA

After a long winter, your Hershey home's exterior needs attention. Learn the best time to power wash, what to focus on, and how to prepare your property for the warmer months ahead.

Get Your Spring Cleaning Scheduled

Why Spring Power Washing Matters in Hershey PA

Winter in Hershey leaves its mark. Snow, ice, salt spray, and months of debris accumulation create a layer of grime on your driveway, deck, siding, and roof. Spring power washing isn't just about curb appeal—it's about protecting your home's exterior from damage and extending the life of your surfaces.

In Hershey, where we experience freeze-thaw cycles and significant winter weather, spring cleaning is critical. Mildew, algae, and moss growth accelerate after moisture-heavy winters, and salt residue can damage concrete and pavers if left unchecked.

When to Power Wash Your Home in Hershey

Timing matters. Late April through May is ideal for spring power washing in the Hershey area:

  • All frost has melted – Cleaning frozen or partially frozen surfaces can cause damage
  • Pollen season is winding down – You'll see cleaner results if you wait past peak pollen
  • Before summer entertaining – Get your outdoor spaces guest-ready
  • Early enough for regrowth prevention – Spring cleaning prevents algae and mold buildup throughout summer

Pro Tip: Schedule Early

Power washing contractors in Lancaster County get busy in late April and May. If you want flexibility in scheduling, call in early April to book your spring appointment.

What to Power Wash First: Hershey Home Priority List

You don't have to power wash everything at once. Here's what to prioritize first:

  1. Driveway – Winter salt and dirt accumulate heavily. Power washing your driveway is the fastest way to refresh your curb appeal and prevent salt damage.
  2. Deck – Spring is the perfect time to clean before you start using it again. Pressure washing removes winter debris, mold, and mildew, preparing it for sealing.
  3. Patio and outdoor pavers – Same benefits as your deck, plus you remove weed growth between pavers.
  4. House siding – Soft wash your siding to remove mold and mildew without risk of damage.
  5. Roof cleaning – If you see dark streaks or moss growth, a soft wash removes it and extends roof life.

Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing for Spring Cleaning

Not every surface gets pressure washed. Here's the breakdown:

Pressure Wash (high PSI): Concrete, pavers, asphalt driveways, decks (certain materials), patios. Removes stubborn salt, dirt, and stains.

Soft Wash (low PSI): Siding, shingles, stucco, delicate surfaces. Removes mold, mildew, and algae without damage.

A spring cleaning plan combines both methods for maximum protection and results.

How to Prepare Your Hershey Property for Spring Washing

Before we arrive, a few steps help the process go smoothly and ensure maximum safety and results:

  • Clear deck and patio areas of furniture and potted plants
  • Move vehicles out of the driveway and any visible area
  • Close windows and secure any outdoor fixtures to prevent overspray moisture
  • Remove loose debris (leaves, branches, ice dams) from gutters and roof edges
  • Mark any delicate areas, landscaping, or outdoor electrical outlets
  • Notify us of any recent repairs or sensitive plants needing protection

Spring Maintenance Tips After Power Washing

Getting your home clean is the first step. Keeping it clean requires maintenance throughout the spring and summer:

  • Seal your driveway – After cleaning, apply a sealant to prevent salt damage next winter and extend the life of the concrete
  • Stain and seal your deck – Spring is the ideal time. It protects wood from UV damage and moisture over the summer season
  • Check gutters and downspouts – Clear any debris to prevent spring runoff damage and foundation issues
  • Plan fall and winter cleanings – This prevents buildup that makes next year's cleaning more expensive and time-consuming

Spring Power Washing Timeline and Scheduling

If you want to get your Hershey home ready for spring entertaining, timing is critical. Here's a recommended timeline:

  • Early April: Contact us for a quote and schedule your appointment. Slots fill quickly.
  • Late April–May: Power washing takes place. Expect 4–8 hours depending on scope.
  • Immediately after: Drying time. Most surfaces dry within 24 hours in normal weather.
  • Within a week: Apply sealant to driveway and deck (weather permitting).
  • June onward: Enjoy a clean, protected home for the entire summer.

Don't wait until June—spring power washing in Hershey is best done in April and early May before the busy season peaks.

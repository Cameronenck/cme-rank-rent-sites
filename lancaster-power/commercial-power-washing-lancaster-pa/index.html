Commercial Pressure Washing in Lancaster County

Commercial properties face different cleaning challenges than residential — higher foot traffic, grease and oil from food service operations, gum on concrete, loading dock grime, and property management requirements for multi-unit residential complexes. Our commercial crew handles all of it using truck-mounted equipment capable of high-volume output that residential equipment can't match.

We work with property managers, business owners, shopping center maintenance teams, and HOAs throughout Lancaster County. Flexible scheduling including early morning, evening, and weekend slots to minimize disruption to your operations.

Commercial Services We Provide

  • Storefront and building exterior washing — concrete block, brick, EIFS, and metal panel facades
  • Parking lot and parking garage cleaning — oil stains, gum, tire marks, and general grime
  • Loading dock and dumpster pad cleaning — grease, organic waste, and heavy soiling
  • Sidewalk and walkway cleaning — gum removal, algae, and staining
  • Awning cleaning — fabric and metal awnings washed safely
  • Multi-unit residential exterior — apartment complexes and HOA-managed communities
  • Restaurant exterior and drive-through — grease and food waste from high-traffic food service

Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Many Lancaster County commercial property managers set up quarterly or semi-annual cleaning programs with us. Scheduled maintenance keeps properties consistently clean, avoids the buildup that makes individual cleanings more expensive, and simplifies property management. We provide invoicing, scheduling reminders, and consistent crew assignments for recurring clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work after hours or early morning for Lancaster businesses?
Yes. We accommodate business schedules. Early morning before opening, late evening after close, and weekend slots are all available for commercial clients. We coordinate timing with your operations team upfront.
Do you handle grease trap areas and restaurant exterior cleaning?
Yes — restaurant exterior cleaning, including grease around kitchen vents, dumpster pads, and drive-through lanes, is a service we provide. Grease requires specific degreasers and hot water capability. Our truck-mounted equipment handles it.
Can you provide certificates of insurance for property management?
Yes. We carry full commercial general liability insurance and can provide certificates of insurance to property managers, HOAs, or landlords as required.

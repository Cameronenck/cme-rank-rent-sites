LOCAL EXPERTISE

Lancaster's Stucco, Brick, and Stone Homes:
Why Soft Washing Beats High-Pressure Every Time

Lancaster County's historic architecture needs a careful approach. Here's what we've learned from 19+ years in the county.

Historic Stucco Needs Gentle Care

Many Lancaster homes have original or repaired stucco that cracks or spalls under high water pressure. Soft washing cleans the surface without forcing water into the substrate or dislodging old mortar — preserving the home's character and structural integrity.

Mold and Algae in PA Humidity

Pennsylvania's humid summers create ideal conditions for biological growth on north-facing walls, shaded areas, and rooflines. Our cleaning formulas kill mold and algae at the root — not just rinse them away — so your home stays clean far longer between services.

Hard Water Mineral Deposits

Lancaster County's water supply runs through limestone and karst geology, which means many homes develop white mineral streaking on driveways, patios, and siding. We use targeted solutions to dissolve these deposits without pitting the underlying surface.

Pre-Paint Surface Prep

If you're planning to paint or stain a surface, proper washing is the first step — paint bonds better and lasts longer on a clean substrate. We prepare siding, decks, and trim for painting projects across Lancaster County, saving homeowners money on early repaints.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does power washing cost in Lancaster PA?
Typical ranges for Lancaster County homes: house washing $150–$400, driveway cleaning $80–$200, deck washing $100–$300, roof cleaning $300–$600, commercial building exteriors vary by size. We provide free on-site estimates for every job so you know the exact price before we start.
What is soft washing and is it really safe for my roof?
Soft washing uses low water pressure combined with eco-friendly cleaning solutions to lift and kill biological growth like mold, algae, and mildew. For asphalt shingles especially, it's the recommended method because high-pressure washing strips granules from the shingles and shortens their lifespan. Our low-pressure approach cleans the roof without any physical damage to the material.
How often should I wash my house in Lancaster PA?
Most Lancaster County homes benefit from washing every one to two years. Homes with heavy tree cover, those facing north, or homes in shaded valleys tend to accumulate mold and algae faster due to Pennsylvania's humid summers. If you're noticing green or black streaking, that's algae and it's time for a cleaning regardless of the schedule.
Is pressure washing safe for old brick and stucco?
Not always. Traditional high-pressure washing can force water into old mortar joints, erode soft stucco, and cause spalling in aged brick. Lancaster County has a lot of historic and older homes that require a gentler approach. We use soft washing on these materials — low pressure with cleaning chemistry that does the work instead of water force.
Do I need to be home during the service?
Not necessarily. We need access to an outdoor water spigot and a brief walkthrough at the start to confirm which areas to clean. Many customers head to work after the initial walkthrough. We'll confirm all details at scheduling and let you know when the job is complete.
Do you serve areas outside Lancaster city?
Yes. We serve all of Lancaster County including Lititz, Ephrata, Manheim, Mount Joy, Elizabethtown, Millersville, Columbia, Quarryville, Strasburg, Bird-in-Hand, Leola, New Holland, Akron, Denver, and Terre Hill. Call us to confirm if you're outside these towns.

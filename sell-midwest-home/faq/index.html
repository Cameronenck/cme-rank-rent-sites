Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about selling your home for cash in the Midwest

How does selling my house for cash work?
You contact us with your property details, we evaluate it and present a fair cash offer within 24 hours. If you accept, we handle all paperwork and close on your timeline -- as fast as 7 days or on a date that works for you.
How fast can you close?
We can close in as little as 7 days. If you need more time, we work on your schedule. There are no deadlines or pressure to move faster than you are comfortable with.
Do I need to make repairs before selling?
No. We buy houses in any condition -- outdated kitchens, roof damage, foundation issues, hoarding situations, fire damage, and more. You do not need to spend a dollar on repairs.
Are there any fees or commissions?
None. There are no real estate agent commissions, no closing costs to you, and no hidden fees. The cash offer you accept is the amount you receive at closing.
What states do you buy houses in?
We buy houses across Illinois, Ohio, Indiana, Michigan, Missouri, and Kentucky. If your property is in the Midwest, we can likely make an offer.
How do you determine your offer price?
We evaluate the property's condition, location, comparable sales in the area, and current market conditions. Our goal is a fair offer that reflects the true value while accounting for any needed repairs.
Can I sell my house if I am behind on payments?
Yes. We work with homeowners facing foreclosure, tax liens, and delinquent payments. Selling for cash can help you avoid foreclosure and protect your credit.
What if my house has tenants?
We buy tenant-occupied properties. Whether you have cooperative or difficult tenants, we handle the transition. You do not need to evict anyone before selling.
Is your cash offer obligating?
No. Our offer is completely no-obligation. You can take time to think it over, compare options, and decide with no pressure. If it does not work for you, there is no cost.
How do I get started?
Call us at 312-815-2536 or fill out the form on our website. We will ask a few questions about your property and present a cash offer within 24 hours.

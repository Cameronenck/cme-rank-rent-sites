Immediate Safety After a Storm in Lebanon PA

After high winds, ice storms, or severe weather hits Lebanon PA, your first instinct might be to grab a chainsaw and start cleanup. Don't. Storm-damaged trees are unpredictable and dangerous.

Why DIY Storm Cleanup is Risky

  • Tension in the wood: Fallen or partially uprooted trees carry internal stress. Cut in the wrong spot and the wood can snap or spring violently, causing serious injury.
  • Hidden hazards: Power lines, debris, and unstable limbs may not be visible. Professional arborists identify these before cutting.
  • Equipment requirements: Large storm-damaged trees in Lebanon PA typically need heavy equipment, cranes, or specialized rigging — not homeowner chainsaws.
  • Liability: If someone is injured during your cleanup, you're liable. Professionals carry insurance and assume that risk.

The smart move: Call a professional tree removal service in Lebanon PA immediately after a storm. It's not just safer — it's often cheaper than dealing with injuries or property damage.

When to Call Emergency Tree Removal in Lebanon PA

Not every fallen branch needs emergency service. Here's when you should call right away:

Call Immediately If:

  • A tree has fallen on your house, garage, or shed
  • Tree limbs are touching or near power lines
  • A tree is partially uprooted but still standing (widowmakers — extremely dangerous)
  • A large tree is leaning against another tree or structure at an angle
  • Branches are hanging over roadways, making the road hazardous
  • The tree is blocking your driveway and you need emergency access

Lebanon PA experiences harsh winters and spring thunderstorms. When power lines are involved or a tree poses immediate danger to life or property, call emergency services right away. Professional arborists have the equipment and training to handle what homeowners cannot.

Can Wait (24–48 hours):

  • Small branches or limbs down in your yard, not blocking structures
  • Trees with damage but still standing and stable
  • Multiple fallen trees with no immediate hazard

Even non-emergency work should be scheduled within 48 hours. Trees left too long can complicate cleanup and invite pest damage.

Preparing Your Property for Future Storms

After a storm cleanup, consider preventive care to reduce risk next time:

  • Tree pruning: Remove dead branches, thin dense canopies, and improve wind resistance. This reduces storm damage by 30–40%.
  • Remove weak trees: Diseased, leaning, or hollow trees are the first to fall in a storm. Removal now prevents damage later.
  • Cable bracing: For high-value trees with multiple stems or codominant branches, cabling can prevent splitting.
  • Regular inspections: Annual arborist inspections catch early signs of disease, weakness, or hazard before a storm hits.

Investing in tree care in Lebanon PA pays dividends when the next storm arrives.

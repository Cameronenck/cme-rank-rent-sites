Local Expertise

Lancaster County’s Older Trees: Why Heritage Oaks, Storm-Damaged Elms, and Pennsylvania Winters Make Professional Removal Essential

Lancaster County’s tree canopy is beautiful but complex. Here’s what local homeowners actually face.

Heritage Oak Root Systems

Lancaster County’s mature oaks frequently exceed 80 feet and have root systems that extend well beyond the canopy. Professional rigging and sectional felling is required to drop these trees safely — near homes, fences, and power lines.

Storm-Damaged Elms & Ash

Emerald ash borer has weakened or killed thousands of ash trees across Lancaster County. Dead ash trees become brittle unpredictably — they require immediate assessment and safe removal before the wood fails on its own terms.

Pennsylvania Ice & Wind Events

Lancaster County sees significant ice accumulation events that crack major limbs and topple trees with surprising frequency. A leaning tree after a storm is a time-sensitive hazard — professional assessment determines risk level before the next weather event.

Dense Residential Lots

Many Lancaster Borough and Lititz neighborhoods have homes on tight lots with mature trees growing within feet of structures. This requires crane work or precision rigging that backyard DIY simply cannot safely accomplish.

FAQ

Common Questions About Tree Removal in Lancaster PA

Tree removal costs in Lancaster County depend heavily on tree size, species, location, and access. A small tree (under 30 feet) typically runs $300–$750. A medium tree (30–60 feet) generally costs $800–$1,800. Large trees over 60 feet or those near structures typically run $1,800–$4,500+. Stump grinding is usually priced separately at $150–$400. We give written fixed-price quotes so there are no surprises.

Permit requirements vary by municipality within Lancaster County. The City of Lancaster has a street tree ordinance that may require permits for trees in the right-of-way. Most residential private property tree removals in surrounding townships do not require permits, but we check local ordinances for every job. We handle this research as part of our assessment — you don’t have to navigate it yourself.

All branches and brush are chipped on-site and hauled away. Large trunk sections can be left as firewood if you want them, or we haul those as well. We discuss your preference before starting the job. If you want the wood split and stacked, we can arrange that for an additional fee. The default is full haul-away — your yard is completely clear when we leave.

Signs that typically indicate removal over trimming: significant structural defects (trunk cavities, major cracks), dead sections constituting more than 50% of the canopy, root system damage compromising stability, disease that has spread through the tree’s vascular system, or location where failure would cause property or personal harm. Our ISA-certified arborists assess every tree honestly — if trimming is the right call, we’ll tell you that rather than recommend unnecessary removal.

Yes — we take emergency calls 24/7. If a tree has fallen on your home, vehicle, or utility line, or is actively threatening any of these, call us at 717-297-1693 immediately. We dispatch same-day for active hazards. For non-emergency storm damage, we prioritize scheduling within 24–48 hours.

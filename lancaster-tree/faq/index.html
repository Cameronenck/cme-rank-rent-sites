Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about tree removal and trimming in Lancaster, PA

How much does tree removal cost in Lancaster PA?
Most tree removals in Lancaster County range from $300 to $2,500 depending on tree size, species, location, and access. We provide free on-site estimates before any work begins.
Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Lancaster?
Lancaster City requires permits for removing trees over a certain diameter in some zoning districts. Outside city limits, most townships do not require permits for private property trees. We can help you check requirements.
Do you offer emergency tree removal?
Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency response for storm damage, fallen trees blocking roads or structures, and hazardous limbs throughout Lancaster County.
What is the best time of year to remove a tree?
Late fall through early spring is ideal because trees are dormant, the ground is firmer, and there is less impact on surrounding landscaping. However, we remove trees safely year-round.
Do you grind stumps after removal?
Yes. Stump grinding is available as an add-on or standalone service. We grind stumps 6 to 8 inches below grade and backfill with the wood chips.
Are your arborists certified?
Yes. Our crew includes ISA-certified arborists with 19+ years of experience working specifically with Lancaster County tree species and conditions.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Lancaster, Lititz, Ephrata, Manheim, Mount Joy, Elizabethtown, Strasburg, Quarryville, Columbia, Millersville, New Holland, and surrounding communities.
Can you trim trees near power lines?
We handle trimming near utility lines following ANSI Z133 safety standards. For branches directly touching high-voltage lines, we coordinate with the utility company.
Do you haul away all the wood and debris?
Yes. Our standard service includes full cleanup, chipping of branches, and hauling. If you want to keep firewood-length logs, just let us know.
How long does a tree removal take?
Most single-tree removals take 2 to 6 hours depending on size and complexity. Large removals near structures may take a full day with rigging and crane work.
